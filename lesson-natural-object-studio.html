Unit 1 · Lesson 5

Writing Studio: Natural Object

Plan, draft, and revise a word picture that lets your reader see and feel one natural object.

45–55 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: object description

IN THIS LESSON

1. Choose2. Plan3. Warm up4. Notice5. Draft6. Revise

1. Choose · 5 min

Bring one small piece of nature.

Choose a leaf, stone, feather, shell, pine cone, seed pod, twig, or flower. Hold it, look at it closely, and place it beside you.

Ajuda em português

Escolha um objeto pequeno da natureza. Observe com calma: cor, tamanho, forma, textura, partes, cheiros e sons. Você vai escrever um pequeno retrato do objeto em inglês.

Remember: do not choose an object only because it is “nice.” Choose one that has interesting details.

2. Plan · 8 min

Collect your exact words.

Before you write sentences, collect words that are true about your object. Use English first. The Portuguese help is there when you need it.

Useful English words

colour: pale, dark, golden, grey, speckled · shape: oval, round, curved, pointed · texture: smooth, rough, dry, waxy, soft · comparison: looks like, reminds me of, as … as.

4. Notice · 7 min

Read a small object portrait.

A pine cone

This is a small dark-brown pine cone. It is about as long as my thumb, and it feels rough in my hand. Its dry scales overlap like tiny wooden roofs. Some edges are sharp, but the cone is light. When I shake it, it makes a soft sound. It reminds me of a little tower from the forest.

Which sentence creates a word picture?

Notice the order: name the object → describe it exactly → add a sensory detail → create one image.

5. Draft · 12–15 min

Write your object portrait.

Guided path: write 5–6 sentences. Core path: write 7–9 connected sentences. Your description should be true to the object, but one imaginative comparison can make it memorable.

Sentence starters — use them only if you need them:
This is a … · It is … and … · It has … · It feels … · When I … · It looks like … / It reminds me of …
0 words

6. Revise & reflect · 6 min

Read it like a writer.

Read your work aloud once. Then revise one sentence to make it more exact or more vivid.

Unit 1 completeObserve & Describe

You now have a finished portfolio piece: a natural-object description written with close observation and your own voice.

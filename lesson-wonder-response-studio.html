Unit 2 · Lesson 5

Writing Studio: Wonder Response

Bring your ideas together in one clear, personal paragraph.

45–55 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: Unit 2 response

IN THIS LESSON

1. Choose2. Plan3. Review4. Study a model5. Draft & revise6. Finish

1. Choose · 5 min

Choose one real source of wonder.

It can be grand or small. What matters is that you can describe it and explain why it matters to you.

Choose one: a place in nature · an animal · a sky or weather moment · a small detail you noticed · a wish for a better future connected to nature
Ajuda em português

Esta é sua produção final da Unidade 2. Escolha algo real que desperte seu interesse ou encantamento. Primeiro organize as ideias; depois escreva um parágrafo. Você pode usar os modelos para a estrutura, mas não copie as ideias.

2. Plan · 10 min

Give your paragraph a path.

Use short notes first. You do not need complete sentences in the planning boxes.

3. Controlled review · 10 min

Build the useful sentences first.

Sentence 1 of 2 · image + feeling

Put the words here

Sentence 2 of 2 · explanation + closing idea

Put the words here

4. Study a model · 8 min

Notice how the paragraph moves.

A tree after rain

I feel wonder when I look at a tree after rain. I notice bright drops hanging from its dark green leaves. The air is cool, and the branches move quietly in the wind. This small scene makes me feel calm because it asks me to slow down. Also, it reminds me that nature changes every day. In the end, a simple tree can help me see that ordinary moments are important.

Its path: source of wonder → exact details → feeling → because → another thought → closing idea.

Which sentence explains why the moment matters?

5. Create · 15–20 min

Draft, then revise.

Guided path: write 5–6 sentences. Core path: write 80–120 words. Include an exact detail, a feeling, and a reason.

Useful starters:
I feel wonder when… · I notice… · It makes me feel… because… · Also,… · In the end,…

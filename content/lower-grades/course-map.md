# Lower Grades Course Map

## Course purpose

This overview maps a Waldorf-inspired English-as-an-additional-language pathway for Grades 1 to 6. It is not an official Waldorf curriculum. It describes common teaching tendencies and translates them into a future build plan for this site.

The lower grades move from oral immersion toward conscious literacy:

- **Grades 1–3:** speech, listening, imitation, song, verse, rhythm, gesture, games, stories, and practical classroom language.
- **Grades 4–6:** reading known material, copywork, dictation, illustrated vocabulary books, grammar in context, paragraphs, retellings, letters, summaries, and reports.

## Grade 1 — Listening, Imitation & Joyful Speech

**Focus:** greetings, names, colours, numbers, classroom objects, body parts, weather, animals, family words, action verbs, and seasonal language.

**Method:** songs, finger plays, circle games, echo speaking, picture-supported stories, gestures, and movement.

**Portfolio seed:** an illustrated song or class verse page.

## Grade 2 — Verses, Fables & Useful Phrases

**Focus:** daily routines, opposites, simple descriptions, animals and their qualities, movement words, food, clothing, days, months, and polite exchanges.

**Method:** call-and-response dialogues, fable retellings, action poems, guessing games, and repeated question patterns.

**Portfolio seed:** a small illustrated animal word collection.

## Grade 3 — Practical Life, Work & First Sentences

**Focus:** home, garden, farming, building, professions, directions, time, simple questions, present-tense actions, and classroom instructions.

**Method:** role play, practical commands, rhythmic question chains, copied familiar sentences, and reading from known oral material.

**Portfolio seed:** a labelled picture of a home, farm, or craft process.

## Grade 4 — Reading Known Texts & Naming Grammar

**Focus:** nature descriptions, local geography, animals, directions, simple paragraphs, nouns, verbs, adjectives, pronouns, plurals, and sentence order.

**Method:** recitation, short dictation, copywork, illustrated vocabulary books, simple reading, and grammar noticed inside meaningful sentences.

**Portfolio seed:** a short animal or landscape description.

## Grade 5 — Myths, Descriptions & Connected Writing

**Focus:** ancient cultures, journeys, biographies, comparisons, regular and common irregular verbs, past tense, conjunctions, and paragraph flow.

**Method:** story retelling, short letters, oral presentations, reading for gist and detail, and supported revision.

**Portfolio seed:** a myth retelling or travel postcard.

## Grade 6 — Grammar, Reports & Clear Paragraphs

**Focus:** history and geography themes, biographies, science observations, modal verbs, past and future forms, questions, clauses, summaries, and short reports.

**Method:** dictation, grammar practice from meaningful texts, research notes, structured paragraphs, recitation, dialogues, and presentations.

**Portfolio seed:** a factual report with an illustration or map.

## Build order

1. Grade 4 Bridge: independent lower-grade web practice starts where learners can read and write simple English with support.
2. Grades 5–6: expand connected writing, grammar in context, reading response, biography, geography, and report-writing.
3. Grades 1–3: create teacher-led and parent-supported materials with audio, printable prompts, picture cards, gestures, songs, and games.

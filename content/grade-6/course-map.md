# Grade 6 Course Map

## Course purpose

Grade 6 bridges lower-grade oral and written foundations into the more independent Grade 7 pathway. Learners practise clear paragraphs, grammar in context, short biography, geography and science observation, and factual report writing.

The work should remain concrete and portfolio-friendly: notes, maps, timelines, labelled illustrations, careful paragraphs, and short reports.

## Support routes

- **Guided A2+:** word banks, sentence frames, model paragraphs, Portuguese rescue notes, and shorter writing.
- **Core B1:** one focused paragraph or short report section with planning and revision.
- **Stretch B1+:** evidence, source notes, careful modal verbs, and a polished portfolio page.

## Unit 1 - Paragraph Craft

**Outcome:** Write one clear factual paragraph from notes.

1. From Notes to a Clear Paragraph - released in `lower-lessons.html`
2. Topic Sentences that Guide the Reader
3. Details in a Sensible Order
4. Transitions without Overloading
5. Writing Studio: One Polished Paragraph

## Unit 2 - A Person in Their Time

**Outcome:** Write a two-paragraph biography with time, place, work, challenge, and significance.

1. A Person in Their Time - released in `lower-lessons.html`
2. Timeline Notes to Sentences
3. Past Tense Review in Biography
4. May Have, Could Have, Probably
5. Writing Studio: Two-Paragraph Biography

## Unit 3 - Science and Geography in Words

**Outcome:** Separate observation from interpretation and explain place, cause, and result.

1. Observation, Cause, and Result - released in `lower-lessons.html`
2. Map Language: North, South, Across, Along
3. Compare Two Places Carefully
4. What the Evidence Shows
5. Writing Studio: Observation Report

## Unit 4 - Clear Factual Reports

**Outcome:** Create a one-page factual report with headings, paragraphs, and a labelled visual.

1. Report Page with Map or Illustration - released in `lower-lessons.html`
2. Headings that Organise Information
3. Source Notes in Simple English
4. Revise for Exact Verbs and Clear Pronouns
5. Final Portfolio: Factual Report Page

## Portfolio outcome

A complete Grade 6 portfolio should include:

- one revised factual paragraph from notes
- one two-paragraph biography
- one observation report
- one report page with a map, timeline, labelled drawing, or process sketch

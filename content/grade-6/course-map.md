# Grade 6 Course Map

## Course purpose

A self-paced English reinforcement block for Brazilian Grade 6 learners. It uses Waldorf Grade 6 mathematics themes, especially business math, proportion, percentages, money, interest, budgeting, and precise geometry, as the context for English practice.

The block is not a complete official Waldorf curriculum. It supports school learning by helping students use English to read prices, explain calculations, compare choices, describe steps, and write short practical texts.

## Block 1 - Fair Trade, Numbers & Clear Words

**Outcome:** a small illustrated market-day portfolio with prices, discounts, profit/loss, a budget, one geometry instruction, and one short business message.

| Lesson | Title | Math context | English focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Numbers in the Real World | prices, quantities, totals | costs, total, each, altogether |
| 1.2 | Fractions, Decimals & Percentages | equivalent forms | one half, 50%, 0.5, percent of |
| 1.3 | A Fair Discount | sale prices | cheaper, save, discount, because |
| 1.4 | Better Deal? | comparison and unit price | more than, less than, per, better deal |
| 1.5 | Cost, Price & Profit | profit and loss | cost, sell, earn, lose, profit |
| 1.6 | Interest & Borrowing Carefully | simple interest | borrow, lend, pay back, fair, unfair |
| 1.7 | Budget Challenge | fixed budget and choices | I can afford, I cannot afford, choose |
| 1.8 | Geometry with Precision | lines, angles, circles | draw, mark, measure, divide, meet |
| 1.9 | Business Writing Studio | order/ad/message | polite request, short advert, receipt |
| 1.10 | Market Day Portfolio | mixed review | explain my plan and reflect |

## Lesson rhythm

Each lesson follows a shared rhythm:

1. Scenario
2. Key words with Portuguese support
3. Interactive math-English practice
4. Sentence builder
5. Short writing task
6. Reflection and confidence check

## Support model

- **Guided / A1+-A2:** bilingual glossary, matching, sentence frames, one or two calculations.
- **Core / A2:** complete the full interactive task and write 3-5 sentences.
- **Stretch / B1:** explain a choice, compare options, or add one ethical/practical reason.

All learners work with the same real-world subject. The routes change the amount of language support, not the dignity of the task.

# Grade 8 Course Map

## Course purpose

A self-paced English reinforcement course for Brazilian learners aged about 13-14. It follows the same pattern as Grade 7: meaningful Waldorf classroom themes lead the work, and English practice helps learners read, explain, compare, argue, and write with increasing independence.

This Grade 8 plan uses Jamie York's Grade 8 mathematics themes as a major organising context. The learner practises English through number systems, algorithms, percent growth, dimensional analysis, proportions, mensuration, stereometry, loci, conic sections, and mathematical explanation. It is not a replacement for a mathematics course; it is English practice built around rich Grade 8 material.

Each lesson offers Guided, Core, and Stretch paths. The topic stays shared; the amount of language support changes.

## What Jamie York Teaches in Grade 8

The Grade 8 math content named in Jamie York Press curriculum overviews includes:

- number bases: ancient number systems, expanded decimal notation, scientific notation, binary, octal, hexadecimal, and arithmetic in different bases;
- square root algorithm and Pythagorean Theorem calculations;
- percents and growth: finding the base, increase/decrease, exponential growth, growth formula, and the rule of 72;
- dimensional analysis: metric and U.S. conversions, rates, area and volume conversions, and density;
- proportions and ratios, including word problems and rate problems;
- algebra: laws of exponents, order of operations, evaluating expressions, distributive property, equations with fractions, and repeating decimals;
- computer thinking: bits, bytes, ASCII, and algorithms written in English;
- mensuration: area, volume, surface area, circle measures, solids, and dimensional reasoning;
- stereometry: Platonic and Archimedean solids, nets, duality, orthogonal views, Euler's formula, and spatial imagination;
- loci: circle, parallel-line, perpendicular-bisector, angle-bisector, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola, conic sections, Cassini curves, and curves in movement;
- puzzle problems.

Sources used for planning: Jamie York Press, "A Grades 1-8 Math Curriculum Overview"; Jamie York Press, "Math Curriculum Overview - grades 6-8"; Jamie York Press, "8th Grade Stereometry and Loci Lesson Plans."

### Unit 8 support model

- **Guided / B1:** bilingual glossary, shorter texts, sentence frames, diagrams, and 100-150 word writing tasks.
- **Core / secure B1-B1+:** connected paragraphs, worked examples, source notes, and 150-220 word writing tasks.
- **Stretch / B1+-B2:** compare methods, qualify claims, explain limitations, and write 220-350 words with more independence.

The routes are optional support, not labels for the learner. Every learner works with the same intellectually meaningful subject.

## Assessment and progress

Progress is demonstrated through work, revision, and explanation:

- completion of practices and reflections;
- vocabulary and sentence-building practice;
- clear worked examples;
- unit portfolio pieces;
- mathematical explanations with diagrams;
- final research or presentation piece;
- learner self-assessment.

The site should save completion state and drafts per lesson. It should not automatically score open mathematical writing or creative explanation as right or wrong.

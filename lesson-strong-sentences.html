Unit 1 · Lesson 4

Strong Sentences

Join small ideas so your writing flows clearly and naturally.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: revised description

IN THIS LESSON

1. Notice2. Learn3. Choose4. Build sentences5. Revise6. Check

1. Notice · 5 min

Short sentences can sound choppy.

Read both versions aloud. Which one sounds smoother?

Choppy

I found a leaf. It was dry. It moved in the wind. I put it in my book.

Connected

I found a dry leaf, and it moved in the wind before I put it in my book.

The second version joins related ideas. It does not need to repeat I four times.

Português: Uma frase forte junta ideias relacionadas. Isso deixa o texto mais fluido e evita repetição.

2. Learn · 10 min

Choose the bridge between ideas.

A conjunction is a small linking word. It tells the reader how two ideas connect.

Ajuda em português

and = e, para adicionar uma ideia. but = mas, para mostrar contraste. because = porque, para dar uma razão. after = depois que. while = enquanto. Normalmente usamos uma vírgula quando a frase começa com After… ou While….

One useful pattern

After the rain stopped, the grey stone glistened in the sun.

The time part comes first, then a comma, then the main idea.

5. Revise · 10–12 min

Make your writing flow.

Choose a natural object. Turn the four short sentences below into a 2–3 sentence word picture. Use at least two linking words.

Ideas to join:
I found a stone. · It was grey. · It felt cool. · I kept it because it was smooth.
One possible answer

I found a grey stone, and it felt cool in my hand. I kept it because its surface was smooth.

Other good answers are possible. Your job is to make the connections clear.

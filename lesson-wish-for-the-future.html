Unit 2 · Lesson 1

A Wish for the Future

Learn to share one wish, give clear reasons, and write in your own voice.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: personal response

IN THIS LESSON

1. Wonder2. Learn words3. Build sentences4. Plan5. Write6. Reflect

1. Wonder · 5 min

What do you hope for?

A wish is something you would like to happen. It can be small, personal, or important. There is no single correct answer.

Ajuda em português

Um wish é algo que você gostaria que acontecesse. Hoje você vai escolher um desejo e explicar suas razões. Não existe uma resposta certa: o importante é explicar sua ideia com clareza.

Choose a safe prompt: What dream would you like to realise? · What would you like to learn? · What would make your community better? · Which animal would you be for 48 hours?
Which answer gives a wish?

4. Plan · 7 min

Climb the response ladder.

A connected paragraph has a path. Write a few words in each box before you draft.

Read a model response
A wish to learn

I would like to learn to play the guitar. First, music would give me a calm moment after a busy day. Also, I could share songs with my friends and family. For example, I could practise one simple song and play it at home. In the end, this wish is important to me because it would help me become more patient and confident.

5. Create · 12–15 min

Write in your own voice.

Guided path: 5 sentences, using the response ladder. Core path: 6–8 connected sentences. You may use the model for structure, but your idea and reasons must be your own.

Useful starters:
I would like to… · I would choose… · First,… · Also,… · For example,… · In the end,…

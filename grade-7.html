Wish, Wonder & Word Pictures

Thirty-two free reinforcement activities for learners who want extra practice with vocabulary, grammar, reading, and writing. Use them after class, alongside schoolwork, or when a topic needs another attempt.

Choose what you need today

Extra practice should be focused, not exhausting.

This pathway supports learning already happening at school or with a teacher. It is not an official Waldorf curriculum and does not require every activity to be completed in one sequence.

Three ways to use each practice

Choose a route before you begin.

15–20 minutes

Quick Review

Recall what you know, review the key words or rule, and complete one controlled activity.

25–40 minutes

Core Practice

Complete the vocabulary or reading work, controlled practice, and one short written response.

40–55 minutes

Portfolio Challenge

Complete the full activity, write the extended response, compare with the model, and revise.

Unit 2 · Lesson 4

A Natural Wonder

Read a short poem, notice an image, and explain what makes a moment matter.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: reading response

IN THIS LESSON

1. Read2. Learn words3. Notice4. Build ideas5. Respond6. Reflect

1. Read · 7 min

A small moment can feel important.

William Wordsworth was an English poet who often wrote about nature. Read this short poem slowly. You do not need to understand every word on the first reading.

My Heart Leaps Up — William Wordsworth (1802)

My heart leaps up when I behold
a rainbow in the sky:
So was it when my life began;
So is it now I am a man;
So be it when I shall grow old,
or let me die!
The Child is father of the Man;
And I could wish my days to be
Bound each to each by natural piety.

Ajuda em português

O poeta vê um arco-íris e sente uma alegria repentina. Ele espera continuar sentindo esse encantamento pela natureza quando ficar velho. Você não precisa traduzir o poema palavra por palavra; procure a ideia e a imagem principal.

5. Respond · 12–15 min

Write about a moment of wonder.

Choose a real moment: a rainbow, bird, sunset, storm, mountain, tree, garden, animal, or something small you noticed closely.

Read a model response
A moment after rain

Wordsworth’s poem is about a rainbow that fills the poet with joy. He hopes that nature will always make him feel this way. For me, a garden after rain can create the same feeling of wonder. I notice bright drops on the leaves and a quiet smell in the air. This moment reminds me to slow down and look carefully.

Guided path: Write 5 sentences: name the natural thing, describe it, say how it makes you feel, and explain why. Core path: Write 6–8 sentences and connect your response to one idea in the poem.

Useful starters:
The poem is about… · The image of… makes the poet feel… · For me,… · I notice… · It makes me feel… because…

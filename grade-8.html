Grade 8 plan · Ages 13-14 · B1-B1+ / B2 stretch

Form, Force & Clear Reasoning

A planned English reinforcement pathway built around Grade 8 Waldorf mathematics themes. Learners practise reading, explaining, comparing, and writing through number systems, growth, proportions, algebra, mensuration, stereometry, loci, and mathematical research.

53 planned practices9 units3 support routes

Same pathway pattern

Mathematics gives the context. English gives the learner a voice.

This is not a replacement for Jamie York's mathematics curriculum. It is an English-as-an-additional-language companion that uses rich Grade 8 topics for vocabulary, sentence structure, explanation, evidence, diagrams, and portfolio writing.

Three ways to use each practice

Support changes; the topic stays shared.

Guided B1

With Support

Bilingual glossary, sentence frames, shorter texts, diagrams, and 100-150 word writing tasks.

Core B1-B1+

Clear Practice

Connected paragraphs, worked examples, source notes, and 150-220 word writing tasks.

Stretch B1+-B2

Portfolio Challenge

Compare methods, qualify claims, explain limits, and write 220-350 words with more independence.

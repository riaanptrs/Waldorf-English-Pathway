Unit 2 · Lesson 2

Reasons that Connect

Learn to connect your ideas so that a reader can follow your thinking.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: connected response

IN THIS LESSON

1. Notice2. Learn words3. Choose a linker4. Build a paragraph5. Write6. Reflect

1. Notice · 5 min

A reason needs a bridge.

Compare these two answers. Both have good ideas, but only one helps the reader understand the connection between them.

Ideas in a list

I would choose the forest. It is quiet. I can walk there. I can see birds.

Ideas that connect

I would choose the forest because it is quiet. Also, I can walk there and see birds.

Ajuda em português

Palavras de ligação são como pontes: elas mostram a relação entre as ideias. Because explica o motivo; also acrescenta outra ideia; for example dá um exemplo; in the end encerra o texto.

3. Controlled practice · 9 min

Which bridge fits?

Read the two ideas. Choose the linking phrase that shows their relationship.

1. I would choose a bicycle. It would help me travel without making pollution.

2. I could draw the trees. I could write their names in a notebook.

4. Build a paragraph · 10 min

Put the bridges in order.

A simple personal response often follows this path: choice → reason → another reason → example → closing thought.

5. Create · 12–15 min

Write one connected response.

Choose one prompt. Your reader should be able to see your choice and understand why it matters to you.

Choose one: Where would you like to spend one perfect afternoon? · What useful skill would you like to learn? · What small change would improve your school or neighbourhood?
Read a model response
A perfect afternoon

I would choose to spend one perfect afternoon by the sea. First, I would listen to the waves because their sound helps me feel calm. Also, I could walk slowly and look for shells. For example, I could choose three different shells and make a small picture with them at home. In the end, this simple afternoon would become a peaceful memory.

Guided path: Write 5 sentences. Use because, also, and in the end. Core path: Write 6–8 connected sentences, including a specific example.

Helpful structure:
I would choose… · First, … because… · Also, … · For example, … · In the end, …

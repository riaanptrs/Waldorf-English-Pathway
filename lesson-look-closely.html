Unit 1 · Lesson 1

Look Closely

First, learn the words. Then use them to help another person see what you see.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: Object portrait

IN THIS LESSON

1. Choose2. Learn words3. Notice4. Practise5. Build a sentence6. Write7. Check

1. Choose · 3 min

Be an object detective.

Choose one small natural object: a leaf, stone, feather, shell, pine cone, seed pod, twig, or piece of bark. Put it in front of you. You do not need a “beautiful” object—only something real.

Português: Escolha um objeto pequeno da natureza. Hoje você vai aprender palavras para descrevê-lo antes de escrever.

2. Learn words · 10 min

Words give you a way to notice.

Read each group aloud. Choose words that are true for your object. You do not need to memorise every word.

3. Notice · 5 min

Look, touch, and listen.

Now observe silently for one minute. Make notes with single words or short phrases. There are no perfect answers.

4. Practise · 7 min

Choose words that fit.

Which words could describe a stone?

Which sentence helps us see the stone?

Drag the word cards into the box. On a phone or tablet, tap a card to move it. You can also use the keyboard: Tab to a card, then Enter. Complete all three before you write your own description.

Notice the patterns: This + object + is + adjective. · The + object + feels + adjective + and + adjective. · This is a + size + texture + colour + object.
Try saying one pattern about your own object before you write.

6. Write · 10–12 min

Make an object portrait.

Guided: write 4–5 sentences. Challenge: write 6–8 sentences and add a comparison or a sound.

Model: a stone

This is a small grey stone. It is round and smooth. Three thin white lines cross one side. It feels cool in my hand. It looks like a tiny moon.

Use these frames if you need them:
This is a… · It is… · It has… · It feels… · It is about as ___ as… · It looks like / reminds me of…

7. Check & Reflect · 3 min

Before you leave

Answer key — unlocked after completion

Controlled practice

Words that could describe a stone: smooth, rough, round, pointed. Different stones can have different textures and shapes. Hungry does not describe a stone.

Sentence that creates the clearest picture: The smooth grey stone has three white lines across it, like quiet waves.

Sentence builders

  1. This leaf is small and pale green.
  2. The stone feels smooth and cool in my hand.
  3. This is a small rough brown pine cone.

Writing task

The object portrait has more than one possible correct answer. Compare your work with the model, then improve one word or sentence rather than copying it.

Grade 6 · Ages 11-12 · A2+ to B1

Grammar, Reports & Clear Paragraphs

Grade 6 is the bridge between lower-grade writing and the more independent Grade 7 pathway. Learners practise grammar inside meaningful texts, turn notes into paragraphs, write short biographies, observe carefully, and create factual report pages with maps or illustrations.

Bridge year

Make thinking visible in English.

This course is designed for learners who can write simple sentences and are ready to organise ideas. The work stays concrete: history, geography, nature study, biography, science observations, maps, diagrams, and carefully revised paragraphs.

Three support routes

Choose the amount of help, not a different topic.

Guided A2+

Frame First

Use word banks, sentence starters, model paragraphs, and Portuguese rescue notes.

Core B1

Write Clearly

Plan, draft, and revise one focused paragraph or short report section.

Stretch B1+

Add Evidence

Combine notes, qualify claims, cite a source, and create a polished portfolio page.

Portfolio outcome

One factual report page with a labelled visual.

By the end of Grade 6, the learner should be able to choose a concrete topic, collect notes, organise ideas under headings, write clear paragraphs, add a map or illustration, and revise for accuracy, transitions, and sentence control.

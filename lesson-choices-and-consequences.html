Unit 2 · Lesson 3

Choices and Consequences

Share an opinion respectfully and think about what might happen after a choice.

40–45 minutesGuided A2 · Core B1Portfolio: opinion paragraph

IN THIS LESSON

1. Notice2. Learn words3. Choose carefully4. Build sentences5. Write6. Reflect

1. Notice · 5 min

A good opinion leaves room for other people.

You can have a strong opinion without saying that other people are wrong. Use respectful language: explain what you think and give a reason.

Too strong

Everyone should walk to school. Cars are terrible.

Respectful and clear

In my opinion, walking to school is a good choice because it can reduce traffic. However, some students live too far away to walk.

Ajuda em português

Você pode expressar uma opinião forte com respeito. In my opinion mostra que é sua opinião. However mostra que existe outro lado ou uma exceção. Consequence é algo que pode acontecer depois de uma escolha.

5. Create · 12–15 min

Give a fair opinion.

Choose one: Should your school have a quiet reading corner? · Should there be more trees or flowers near your home? · Should students have one screen-free hour each day?
Read a model response
A greener playground

In my opinion, our school should plant more trees near the playground because they would give students shade on hot days. Also, trees can make the space feel calmer and more beautiful. However, young trees need water and care at first. For example, each class could help water one tree every week. In the end, the result could be a cooler playground that everyone can enjoy.

Guided path: Write 5 sentences using In my opinion, because, and However. Core path: Write 6–8 sentences and include one possible consequence.

Helpful structure:
In my opinion, … because … · Also, … · However, … · For example, … · In the end, the result could be …

How this block works

Use numbers to say something clearly.

Grade 6 Waldorf math often becomes practical: prices, percent, fairness, business, and exact forms. Here, the math is the context. The real work is learning to explain a small calculation in English.

Português: A matemática ajuda você a praticar inglês de modo concreto. Primeiro entenda as palavras. Depois calcule. Por fim, explique em uma frase simples.

1.2 · Fractions, Decimals & Percentages

Three forms can show the same amount.

One half, 0.5, and 50% can describe the same part of a whole.

Which set is equivalent?

Sentence frame: 50% of R$20 is R$10 because 50% means one half.

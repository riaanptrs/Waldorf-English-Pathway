Lower Grade Lessons

These are ready-to-use lesson outlines, not only a map. Grades 1-3 are teacher or parent led and mostly oral. Grades 4-5 begin to work more independently with reading, copywork, grammar noticing, and short writing.

20 lesson outlines15-40 minutes1-5 grades

How to use these

Keep the language alive before asking children to explain it.

For younger children, the adult says the English with gesture, picture, object, rhythm, and repetition. The child does not need to translate every word. Portuguese support is for the adult or for a quick rescue when meaning is lost.

Grade 1 · Ages 6-7

Listening, imitation, and joyful speech.

1.1

15-20 min · oral circle

Good Morning Circle

Aim: greet, answer with a name, count classmates, and move with simple commands.

Materials: soft ball or beanbag, space to stand in a circle.

  1. Opening rhythm: clap twice and say, "Good morning, good morning, hello to you."
  2. Pass the ball: "My name is..." Each child repeats only their own name sentence.
  3. Counting step: step around the circle and count slowly from 1 to 12.
  4. Movement commands: stand up, sit down, turn around, clap, jump, stop.
  5. Closing whisper: "Goodbye, goodbye, see you soon."
HelloMy name is...Stand upStop

Portuguese support: Cumprimentos e comandos simples. Nao cobre leitura nem escrita.

Portfolio seed: child draws the circle and adult writes "Good morning".

1.2

15-20 min · colours and nature

Colour Walk

Aim: hear and say colour words through real objects.

Materials: leaves, stones, flowers, cloth pieces, crayons.

  1. Lay out objects and name colours: green leaf, brown stone, yellow flower.
  2. Children touch or point when they hear: "Find something green."
  3. Teacher chants: "Red, yellow, green, and blue, I can see them, can you?"
  4. Children choose one object and say: "It is green" or repeat after teacher.
  5. Draw one object and colour it carefully.
redyellowgreenblueIt is...

Portuguese support: Use objetos reais antes de cartoes ou palavras escritas.

Portfolio seed: colour drawing labelled by the adult.

1.3

20 min · body and movement

Head, Hands, Feet

Aim: learn body words through gesture and rhythm.

Materials: open floor space.

  1. Teacher points and says: head, hands, feet, knees, eyes, ears.
  2. Children copy silently first, then speak with the teacher.
  3. Rhythm: "Hands can clap. Feet can tap. Eyes can look. Ears can hear."
  4. Action game: teacher says one body part; children touch it.
  5. Slow challenge: teacher says two actions: "Clap hands. Tap feet."
headhandsfeeteyesearsclap

Portuguese support: O corpo mostra o significado. Traduza apenas se a crianca se perder.

Portfolio seed: simple body outline with 3 adult-written labels.

1.4

20 min · seasonal speech

Weather Window

Aim: notice the weather and speak one simple sentence.

Materials: window view, small weather drawings.

  1. Look outside quietly for ten seconds.
  2. Teacher says: "Today is sunny/cloudy/rainy/windy."
  3. Children make weather gestures: sun arms, rain fingers, wind hands.
  4. Call and response: "How is the weather?" "It is sunny."
  5. Choose a weather card and place it near the date or class board.
sunnycloudyrainywindyToday is...

Portuguese support: Ligue a fala ao ceu real do dia.

Portfolio seed: one weather picture for the seasonal folder.

Grade 2 · Ages 7-8

Verses, fables, and useful phrases.

2.1

20 min · fable qualities

The Quick Hare and the Slow Tortoise

Aim: use animal words and opposite qualities.

Materials: two simple animal drawings or toys.

  1. Show the hare and tortoise. Say: "The hare is quick. The tortoise is slow."
  2. Children move like each animal: quick steps, slow steps.
  3. Tell a tiny version: "The hare runs. The tortoise walks. The tortoise keeps going."
  4. Call and response: "Who is quick?" "The hare is quick."
  5. Children draw both animals and copy or trace quick/slow if ready.
haretortoisequickslowkeeps going

Portuguese support: quick = rapido; slow = devagar. A moral fica em gestos e historia.

Portfolio seed: animal picture with two quality words.

2.2

20-25 min · daily rhythm

Morning, Noon, Night

Aim: speak simple daily routine phrases.

Materials: three picture cards: morning, noon, night.

  1. Teacher gestures the sun rising, high sun, and moon.
  2. Say and act: "In the morning, I wake up. At noon, I eat. At night, I sleep."
  3. Children place cards in order.
  4. Question chain: "When do you sleep?" "At night."
  5. Draw three small boxes and add one action in each.
morningnoonnightI wake upI sleep

Portuguese support: Use rotina real da crianca; frases curtas sao suficientes.

Portfolio seed: three-part day picture.

2.3

20 min · food and politeness

The Little Picnic

Aim: ask politely for food words in a pretend picnic.

Materials: pretend fruit, bread, cup, basket, cloth.

  1. Name picnic items: apple, banana, bread, water.
  2. Adult models: "May I have an apple, please?" "Here you are."
  3. Children pass items using the phrase with help.
  4. Memory tray: cover the basket and ask, "What is missing?"
  5. Closing: everyone says, "Thank you for the picnic."
applebreadwaterpleasethank you

Portuguese support: May I have...? = Posso pegar/receber...?

Portfolio seed: illustrated picnic word collection.

2.4

20-25 min · description

Animal Guessing Game

Aim: describe animals with colour, size, and action words.

Materials: animal cards or small animal figures.

  1. Review animals: bird, fish, horse, dog, cat, frog.
  2. Teacher gives clues: "It is green. It can jump."
  3. Children guess: "A frog!"
  4. Children choose a card and give one clue with adult support.
  5. Draw one animal and say one sentence about it.
It is...It can...bigsmalljumpswim

Portuguese support: Primeiro modele muitas vezes; depois a crianca da uma pista so.

Portfolio seed: animal clue drawing.

Grade 3 · Ages 8-9

Practical life, work, and first sentences.

3.1

25 min · home and building

Build a House in Words

Aim: name parts of a house and follow practical commands.

Materials: blocks, paper shapes, or drawing materials.

  1. Name parts: door, window, roof, wall, floor.
  2. Build or draw as the adult says: "Put the roof on the house."
  3. Children repeat: "This is the door. This is the roof."
  4. Ask: "Where is the window?" Child points and answers if ready.
  5. Copy one sentence: "This is my house."
doorwindowroofwallThis is...

Portuguese support: Use comandos reais: put, draw, open, close.

Portfolio seed: labelled house drawing.

3.2

25 min · farming

Seed to Bread

Aim: speak a simple process in order.

Materials: seed, soil picture, wheat or grain picture, bread.

  1. Show the sequence: seed, plant, grain, flour, bread.
  2. Act the verbs: plant, water, grow, grind, bake, eat.
  3. Say the chain together: "We plant the seed. The seed grows."
  4. Put picture cards in order.
  5. Copy or trace two process words if ready.
seedplantgrainflourbreadgrows

Portuguese support: Conte como processo pratico, nao como lista solta.

Portfolio seed: five-picture process strip.

3.3

25-30 min · directions

Find the Garden Gate

Aim: follow and give simple directions.

Materials: classroom map, small object, chalk or paper path.

  1. Teach commands: go straight, turn left, turn right, stop.
  2. Adult guides a child across the room using commands.
  3. Place a small "gate" and make a path to it.
  4. Child gives one command at a time to guide the adult.
  5. Draw a simple map with arrows.
go straightturn leftturn rightstopgate

Portuguese support: left/right podem ser marcados nas maos para ajudar.

Portfolio seed: arrow map with two direction words.

3.4

25 min · professions

Who Works Here?

Aim: connect workers, tools, and useful verbs.

Materials: pictures of baker, farmer, builder, teacher; tool cards.

  1. Name workers and tools: baker/oven, farmer/seed, builder/hammer, teacher/book.
  2. Match worker to tool.
  3. Say: "The baker bakes bread. The farmer plants seeds."
  4. Mime one job while others guess.
  5. Write or copy one job sentence.
bakerfarmerbuilderteacherbakesplants

Portuguese support: worker = trabalhador/profissao; tool = ferramenta.

Portfolio seed: worker and tool page.

Grade 4 · Ages 9-10

Reading known texts and naming grammar.

4.1

30 min · nature reading

The River Path

Aim: read a short known text, find nouns and verbs, and copy one strong sentence.

Reading:

The river moves past stones and roots. It is cold and clear. A small bird stands near the water. The bird looks, waits, and flies away.

  1. Read once with an adult or audio support.
  2. Underline nouns: river, stones, roots, bird, water.
  3. Circle verbs: moves, stands, looks, waits, flies.
  4. Copy the sentence with the clearest picture.
  5. Draw the river path and label three things.
riverstonerootmoveswaits

Portuguese support: noun = nome de coisa/pessoa/lugar; verb = acao.

Portfolio seed: copied sentence and river drawing.

4.2

30 min · animal description

Describe an Animal Carefully

Aim: write 4-6 clear sentences about one animal.

  1. Choose an animal you know well.
  2. Make a word bank: colour, size, body parts, movement, home.
  3. Use the frame: "The ___ is ___. It has ___. It can ___. It lives ___."
  4. Add one exact detail: not just "big", but "as big as my hand".
  5. Read your description aloud and improve one word.
It has...It can...It lives...careful detail

Portuguese support: Ajude com banco de palavras antes da escrita.

Portfolio seed: short animal description with drawing.

4.3

30-35 min · local geography

Near, Far, Above, Below

Aim: use position words to describe a simple landscape.

Materials: landscape picture or view from a window.

  1. Name the places: hill, road, tree, river, house, sky.
  2. Point and say: "The tree is near the road. The sky is above the hill."
  3. Choose four position words: near, far, above, below, beside, between.
  4. Write 5 sentences about the picture.
  5. Check that each sentence has a capital letter and full stop.
nearfarabovebelowbeside

Portuguese support: Use desenhos para mostrar preposicoes.

Portfolio seed: labelled landscape description.

4.4

30 min · grammar in context

Noun, Verb, Adjective Hunt

Aim: notice three word classes inside meaningful sentences.

Model sentences:

The bright sun warms the garden. A small lizard runs across the wall. The old tree gives cool shade.

  1. Read the sentences aloud.
  2. Mark nouns in one colour, verbs in another, adjectives in another.
  3. Replace one adjective: bright, small, old, cool.
  4. Write two new sentences about your own garden, street, or room.
  5. Check: Did each sentence include a noun and a verb?
nounverbadjectivebrightcool

Portuguese support: adjective = palavra que descreve o nome.

Portfolio seed: two original grammar-colour sentences.

Grade 5 · Ages 10-11

Myths, descriptions, and connected writing.

5.1

35 min · myth retelling

A Gift from the Gods

Aim: retell a myth moment in past tense with a clear beginning, middle, and end.

Story seed:

A young messenger carried a small fire through the dark valley. The people waited in silence. When the light arrived, they saw each other's faces and began to work again.

  1. Read the story seed and mark beginning, middle, end.
  2. Notice past verbs: carried, waited, arrived, saw, began.
  3. Retell the moment orally in your own words.
  4. Write 6-8 sentences. Use at least four past-tense verbs.
  5. Add one sentence about why the gift mattered.
carriedwaitedarrivedsawbegan

Portuguese support: Past tense = acao que ja aconteceu.

Portfolio seed: short myth retelling.

5.2

35 min · journey writing

Postcard from a Journey

Aim: write a connected travel postcard using place details and feeling words.

  1. Choose a journey: desert, river, mountain, city, forest, sea.
  2. Make a word bank: what I saw, heard, touched, ate, felt.
  3. Use postcard form: greeting, three details, one feeling, closing.
  4. Write 80-100 words to a friend or family member.
  5. Revise one vague word: nice, good, big, beautiful.
I saw...I heard...I felt...Dear...From...

Portuguese support: Troque palavras vagas por detalhes concretos.

Portfolio seed: illustrated postcard.

5.3

35-40 min · comparison

Two Ancient Places

Aim: compare two places using simple linking words.

Materials: two pictures of ancient places, temples, homes, roads, or markets.

  1. Look carefully and list what you can see in each picture.
  2. Use comparison frames: "Both places have..." "Only place A has..."
  3. Choose three categories: building, people, nature, work, travel.
  4. Write one paragraph of 6-8 sentences.
  5. Underline two linking words: both, but, also, however, because.
bothbutalsoonlybecause

Portuguese support: Compare uma coisa por vez para evitar confusao.

Portfolio seed: comparison paragraph with two small sketches.

5.4

35 min · biography

A Life in Six Sentences

Aim: write a short biography with time words and past tense.

  1. Choose a person from history, myth study, family, or local community.
  2. Collect notes: born/lived, work, challenge, action, result, why remembered.
  3. Use the frame: "First... Later... During this time... In the end..."
  4. Write six connected sentences.
  5. Check every sentence begins with a capital letter and ends clearly.
was bornlivedworkedlaterin the end

Portuguese support: Biography = texto curto sobre a vida de alguem.

Portfolio seed: six-sentence biography.
